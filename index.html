01 / About

Engineer by training, builder by instinct.

~6 yrs in software

I’m Fabien, a software engineer with a Master’s in Software Engineering. I currently lead development at NeedList.ORG, where I own the stack day to day and ship features end to end.

On the product side I work in TypeScript with React and NestJS. For the intelligence layer I reach for Python, from notebook experiments to production AI and ML workflows. I came up in a Java-first shop and spent years there. That OOP and strict-typing background still shapes how I write code, even though TypeScript is now my daily driver.

I recently completed two programs at Seneca College. IT Project Management gave me the delivery and process side, eight months in. Artificial Intelligence followed with another eight months on machine learning, deep learning, and applied AI. Both feed directly into how I think about shipping software with a model at its core.
